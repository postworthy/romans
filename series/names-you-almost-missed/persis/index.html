Romans 16:12 (ESV) • excerpt

Paul calls her beloved.

Persis is recognized for hard work in the Lord and also for being beloved. Affection and service belong together in this greeting.

Romans 16:12; see source notes

Do not turn the verb into a life story.

The verse does not name her assignment. Its past-tense wording does not prove she had retired, grown old or died.

Romans 16:12; see source notes

A name does not establish an origin story.

Persis can suggest a Persian association. It does not by itself prove her birthplace, ethnicity, migration story or legal status.

Historical context and limits • source notes

Read it within the larger letter.

The call to genuine love and service helps us consider recognition that includes affection for a person, rather than only appreciation of their output.

Much work, remembered with affection

Romans 16:12 calls Persis beloved and remembers that she has worked hard in the Lord. Paul joins affection to recognition of her labor.

The verse does not tell us her age, birthplace, legal status or whether she had retired from service. Even the associations of her name cannot settle her life history. There is enough here to value sustained work without requiring a dramatic achievement or a detailed biography.
