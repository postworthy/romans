Sosipater sends a greeting.

Paul names Sosipater with Lucius and Jason among the sending companions. The greeting itself recognizes his place in this network.

Romans 16:21; see source notes

Sopater is another name to check.

Acts 20:4 names Sopater of Berea, son of Pyrrhus, among Paul’s travel companions. Sosipater and Sopater may refer to one person, but the link is not stated.

Romans 16:21; see source notes

A shorter form is not an identification.

Name forms can invite comparison. They cannot by themselves supply Berean origin, a father’s name or a travel history for the Romans sender.

Scripture & the people behind the names

A person whose greeting survives

Romans 16:21 names Sosipater among the senders of greetings. Paul identifies him with Lucius and Jason as kinsmen, without giving an individual ministry account.

Acts 20:4 names Sopater of Berea. The similar names have prompted suggestions of a connection, but the two should not be merged as an established fact. The surviving greeting is a reason to remember Sosipater without assigning him a hometown or a father from another passage.
