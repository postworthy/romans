Romans 16:5 (ESV) • excerpt

A beginning Paul remembered.

Paul names Epaenetus as the first convert to Christ in Asia. He also calls him beloved. The greeting remembers both a beginning and a person.

Romans 16:5; see source notes

A beginning isn’t the whole story.

Romans greets him among the people in the Roman receiving network. The verse doesn’t tell us when or why he moved, or what happened after his conversion.

Romans 16:5; see source notes

Asia is a particular place.

Here Asia is the Roman province, not the entire modern continent. Some textual traditions read Achaia. Our Scripture card follows the checked ESV: Asia.

Historical context and limits • source notes

Read it within the larger letter.

The gospel reaches peoples and places through particular lives. Epaenetus’s greeting lets us remember an early beginning without measuring the whole harvest.

A remembered beginning

Romans 16:5 names Epaenetus as the first convert to Christ in Asia and calls him beloved. Asia here is the Roman province rather than the whole modern continent. The greeting holds together an early beginning and an enduring relationship.

Paul gives no account of Epaenetus’s conversion or journey to Rome. Nor does he identify him as founder of every church in that province. Remembering a first step need not turn a person into the sole author of everything that followed.
