An opened heart, a welcoming home.

Acts 16:14–15, 40

The Lord opened her heart.

“The Lord opened her heart”

Acts 16:14 (ESV) · excerpt

She was listening beside the river.

Paul and his companions spoke to women gathered at a place of prayer outside Philippi. Lydia heard them, and the Lord opened her heart to attend to Paul’s message.

Acts 16:13–14

She urged them to stay.

After Lydia and her household were baptized, she asked the travelers to come to her house. She wanted them to receive her welcome as someone faithful to the Lord.

Acts 16:15

Two cities belong to her story.

Lydia came from Thyatira and sold purple goods. Luke places the riverside encounter at Philippi, a Roman colony. These details let us notice her working life without assigning her an unrecorded level of wealth.

Acts 16:12–14 · setting and limits

They came back to Lydia.

After leaving prison, Paul and Silas visited Lydia. They saw the believers, encouraged them and departed. Her name appears again within the life of this growing community.

Acts 16:40

Make welcome practical.

Someone visiting your church may need a meal, a place to sit or a person who listens. Ask what would help and offer what you can responsibly share.

A present-day invitation

Whose welcome helped you belong?

Recall one true invitation that made room for you. You may thank that person privately, or share their name with permission.
