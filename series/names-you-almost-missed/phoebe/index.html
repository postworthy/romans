A commended sister who had helped many

Romans 16:1–2 commends Phoebe, identifies her with the church at Cenchreae and asks the Romans to welcome and assist her. Paul recognizes that she had helped many people, including himself. The request makes the church’s response to her a matter of practical fellowship.

Many scholars consider her a likely carrier of the letter, but Paul does not explicitly say she delivered or performed it. The word translated servant also figures in discussion of the office of deacon. Whatever position one takes in that discussion, the text clearly honors her substantial service and asks others to serve her in turn.
