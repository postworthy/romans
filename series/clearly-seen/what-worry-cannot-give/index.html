Worry can fill a day without adding a single hour to your life. Jesus points his hearers toward birds that are fed and flowers that are clothed. Then he reminds them that their heavenly Father knows what they need.

Birds, flowers and the Father’s knowledge

Matthew 6:25–34 asks anxious disciples to consider birds and flowers within Jesus’s teaching about their heavenly Father. The invitation moves toward seeking God’s kingdom and facing the trouble of the present day.

Jesus acknowledges that the day has trouble. A beautiful field is therefore no promise that distress will instantly disappear. The passage directs attention toward the Father who knows his children’s needs. Its reassurance comes from Jesus’s teaching, rather than from scenery alone, and can accompany patient care for someone whose anxiety persists.
