Dora Yu

The grace she wanted remembered

Read the narration

Dora Yu asked her sister not to write her life story if she died suddenly. She'd read a biography of a friend and thought he would have told parts of it differently. When Dora wrote her own testimony, she wanted people to remember the grace she'd needed.

She was a Chinese Christian teacher, already known for encouraging others. A meeting with an old schoolmate brought back a conflict. Dora remembered losing her temper and wrote that God showed her she needed to confess.

She wanted to wait for the other woman to confess first. She was also concerned about her reputation as a Christian worker. In her own account, she says she obeyed.

Dora could speak about God's love while still needing that love herself. In the preface to her testimony, she pictured meeting her Lord with wonder at his grace.

Romans says Christ died for us while we were still sinners. Those who belong to Christ Jesus face no condemnation. We can face our sin honestly, receive mercy and go on serving.

Perhaps someone helped you follow Jesus by admitting their own need for grace. Let their example encourage you to bring your own failures to Christ. His mercy is our hope.

Dora’s own account of grace

Dora Yu’s testimony gives us access to how she wanted her Christian service remembered. In its preface she explains her unease with biographies that might tell a person’s life differently from the way the person would tell it. She includes failures as well as mercies for which she gives thanks.

Chapter V recalls a conflict with a former schoolmate and Dora’s conviction that she needed to confess her own anger. Concern about her standing as a Christian worker made that obedience difficult. Her account does not supply the other woman’s response or the exact words of the confession.

The preface’s exclamation about grace expresses what Dora hoped to say before her Lord; it is not a reported deathbed scene. Her testimony allows us to remember a teacher of the faith as someone who continued to need the Savior’s mercy.

Pause & consider

How has someone’s honesty about their need for grace helped you follow Christ?
