Do I have to say yes to every need?

Romans 12:3-8

Read the narration

Do you feel you have to say yes whenever someone at church needs help?

Romans twelve says that the members of one body don't all have the same function. God has given different gifts, and we belong to one another.

You can take your responsibilities seriously without trying to be every member yourself. Receive the help others can give. Make room for their gifts too.

If you're carrying more than you can manage, talk honestly with a trusted leader. Ask how the work could be shared.

Faithful service grows in a body. You can offer what you've been given while depending on others.

Members who belong to one another

Romans 12:4–8 says the members of one body have different functions and belong to one another. The instruction concerns the faithful use of gifts received by grace. It does not assign every need to a single person.

The book emphasizes service within the church’s shared life. Asking another member for help, acknowledging a limit and receiving care can all serve that life. The application needs both generosity and honest judgment about the responsibility we can actually carry.
