Read the narration

Am I meant to follow Jesus alone?

Romans twelve says we’re one body in Christ, and members of one another. His mercy brings us into a life we share.

Romans fifteen tells us to welcome each other as Christ welcomed us.

Christ didn’t live to please himself. Those who are strong are called to bear with the weak, helping them grow through Scripture.

If you’re still learning, receive help. If you can strengthen someone, make time. Love makes room for people who need patience.

God is forming a people who glorify him with one voice. You’re invited to belong, to be cared for, and to help others grow.

Love brings us into shared life

Romans 12 describes believers as members of one another, each with gifts received by grace. Romans 15:1–7 extends that belonging into bearing burdens and welcoming one another as Christ has welcomed us.

Landon’s chapters follow this movement from mercy to service and shared worship. Receiving help can be part of loving the church as well as offering it. Christ’s welcome gives us reason to make room for people whose needs, strengths and histories differ from our own.

Pause & consider

Where could you make room to give help—or receive it—within your church?
