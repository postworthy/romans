Read the narration

How can I know God’s will for my life?

Romans twelve begins with God’s mercies before it speaks about discernment. Through Jesus Christ, God has shown mercy to people who could never earn it.

Then Paul calls believers to offer their whole lives to God as worship.

Before choosing between opportunities, you can begin here: Lord, I belong to you. How can this choice honor you?

Think about your time, your work, and the people your decision will affect.

You are approaching the God who has already given himself for you in Christ. His mercy gives you a secure place to begin, and a reason to offer him whatever comes next.

The mercies behind the command

Romans 12:1 begins with an appeal grounded in God’s mercies. The instruction to present our bodies follows the letter’s long account of sin, justification, new life and mercy to Jews and Gentiles. The whole person is invited into worship.

Landon’s chapter on a life offered to God keeps that order visible. Discerning God’s will begins within a life already claimed by mercy. Ordinary choices become occasions to respond to the God who has welcomed us through Christ.

Pause & consider

Which ordinary part of your day could become a conscious response to God’s mercy?
