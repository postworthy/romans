How do you know God loves you when life hurts?

Romans five points us to Christ, who died for us while we were still sinners. God moved toward us when we couldn’t make ourselves right.

He reconciled us to himself. And Paul says we now rejoice in God through Jesus Christ.

His gift is a restored relationship with him. You can know him, trust him, and rejoice in him.

Romans eight says suffering can’t separate those in Christ from his love. You can bring him your grief without pretending it doesn’t hurt.

When you wonder whether you’re loved, look again at Jesus. Trust the God who gave his Son and brings you near to himself.

God’s love has a history

Romans 5 points to Christ’s death for sinners as the demonstration of God’s love. Romans 8 then names distress, persecution and danger while affirming that nothing in creation can separate believers from that love in Christ.

These passages give the book’s treatment of assurance its shape. We can acknowledge pain honestly and look to what God has done through his Son. The cross and resurrection give us a place to stand when present circumstances are difficult to interpret.
