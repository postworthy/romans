How can I begin when I do not know my gift?

Romans 12:3-8

Read the narrationGo deeper · context & sources ↓
0:00

Read the narration

What if you want to serve God, but you still don't know your spiritual gift?

Romans twelve names different gifts and tells believers to use them. Service, teaching, generosity and mercy have real people to help.

You can begin by asking your church where help is needed. Let people who know you share what they've noticed. Try a manageable responsibility with their guidance.

Paul's list gives examples of grace at work. It doesn't require you to find one perfect label before you can help.

Pray for wisdom, receive counsel, and take a faithful step. You can learn while serving alongside others.

Begin by serving the person before you

Romans 12:6–8 calls believers to use their differing gifts in ways marked by generosity, diligence and cheerfulness. Paul’s attention is on their exercise within the body.

The book encourages faithful participation in shared life. Someone uncertain about a gift can begin with a real opportunity to serve, listen to others and learn. A label may become clearer as service develops; it need not stand between you and a neighbor who needs care today.

Pause & consider

What modest opportunity to serve could you take before you have a complete account of your gifts?
