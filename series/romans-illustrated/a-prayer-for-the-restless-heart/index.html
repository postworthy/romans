What can I pray when my heart won’t settle?

Romans 15:12–13

Read the narrationGo deeper · context & sources ↓
0:00

Read the narration

Still scrolling, hoping something will settle your heart?

Romans fifteen gives us a prayer: may the God of hope fill you with joy and peace as you believe, so you overflow with hope through the Holy Spirit’s power.

Paul has just pointed to Christ as the hope of the nations. God keeps his promises. Our hope has someone to rest on.

You can bring that prayer into this restless moment. Lord, help me trust you. Fill me with your peace.

You don’t have to manufacture hope by yourself. His Spirit is at work in those who belong to Christ.

Ask him for peace today. And pray this blessing for someone else whose heart is weary.

Paul’s prayer leaves room for a troubled heart

Romans 15:13 asks the God of hope to fill believers with joy and peace in believing, so that they abound in hope by the Holy Spirit’s power. The prayer follows Paul’s account of Christ bringing Jews and Gentiles into shared praise.

The book places this hope within the church’s life and mission. We can borrow Paul’s words on a restless day without turning them into a test of how quickly we become calm. God welcomes a prayer that depends on the strength he supplies.
