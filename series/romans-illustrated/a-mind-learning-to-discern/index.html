How can I tell whether a desire is leading me wisely?

Romans twelve connects discerning God’s will with the renewal of our minds.

The world can teach us to measure every choice by status or comfort. Scripture helps us examine those assumptions in light of what God has done in Christ.

As you consider an opportunity, ask what is shaping your judgment. Are you seeking admiration? Are you making room for love, truth, and service?

Renewal grows as you return to God’s Word and let it correct your thinking. His Spirit is at work in you.

God is patiently forming your judgment. You can keep learning to recognize what pleases him.

Discernment grows through renewal

Romans 12:2 connects discerning God’s will with the renewal of the mind. It follows the appeal to offer our bodies because of God’s mercies. Paul is describing the shaping of a whole life.

The book and sermon ask what is training our desires and assumptions. Scripture and the Spirit’s work help us examine the patterns we absorb in everyday life. The question reaches beyond whether a particular choice is permitted to what kind of person that choice is helping us become.
